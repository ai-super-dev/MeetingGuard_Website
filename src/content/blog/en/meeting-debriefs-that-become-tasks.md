Most meetings end the same way. Someone says "great, let's follow up", everyone leaves, and the action items live on in a summary email nobody opens again.

## The summary already exists

If you use a notetaker, you already get a summary after every call: decisions, owners, next steps. The information is there. It just never becomes work.

## What MeetingGuard will do

From December, MeetingGuard reads the summary your notetaker already sends and turns your action items into tasks:

- Each task gets a due date, said in plain words in the summary or set by you.
- Tasks stay linked to the meeting they came from.
- Reminders use the same alarm system as your meetings.
- Anything overdue is part of your agenda and your daily briefing.

Nothing is saved without you. You see the list of tasks first and confirm the ones you want, just like a meeting card.

## What it does not do

MeetingGuard does not record meetings and does not join your calls. It only reads the summary that your notetaker sends you, after the meeting, when you allow it.

## Until December

You can already create tasks by voice today. Say "Remind me to send the deck to Ana tomorrow" and it is on your list, with a reminder that rings.
