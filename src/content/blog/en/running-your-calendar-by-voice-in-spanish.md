Many voice assistants speak Spanish the way a tourist does: they understand the basics and get lost in real sentences. MeetingGuard was built to work in English and Spanish from day one.

## Talk the way you talk

You do not need special words. Say it the way you would tell a colleague:

- "Comida con Ana el viernes a la 1."
- "Mueve mi reunión de las 15:00 al jueves."
- "Cancela la revisión de diseño de mañana."

MeetingGuard shows a card with what it understood: title, day, time, people and the call link. Nothing is saved until you confirm.

## Switch languages whenever you like

Use the app in Spanish and speak in English to a client, or the other way round. The assistant follows you. Your agenda, your daily briefing and your alarms speak the language you use the app in.

## When it is not sure, it asks

Spanish is rich in ways to say the same time. If you say "a las 3" and you have two meetings that could match, MeetingGuard asks which one you mean before it changes anything. And if it misheard a name, you fix it by typing on the card.

## The same on WhatsApp

Link your WhatsApp number once and write to MeetingGuard in Spanish: "llamada con Mia el jueves a las 10, añade un enlace de Zoom". You get the same card, with Confirm and Cancel buttons in the chat.
